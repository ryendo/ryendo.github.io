I am uploading two PDF tables of Laplacian eigenfunction plots on polygonal domains.  
This post contains one Neumann table and one Dirichlet table, together with a short description.

## PDF

- [Neumann PDF](/files/eigenfunctions/eigenfunctions_table_neumann.pdf)
- [Dirichlet PDF](/files/eigenfunctions/eigenfunctions_table_dirichlet.pdf)

## Short description

- The **Neumann** table gives a visual overview of low and higher modes.
- The **Dirichlet** table makes it easier to inspect how nodal patterns change across modes.
- Both figures are provided as PDFs so that they can be viewed at full size.
